Our Boars

  • Home
  • Our Boars

We have a good selection of purebred Duroc boars, purebred Yorkshire boars, Hamp/Duroc cross boars, and Hamp/York cross boars for sale. Their littermate gilts are also available. We sell our boars for $400 and open gilts for $250.

 

Our Boars are guaranteed breeders!  The boars we sell as breeding stock are the offspring of elite, performance-tested sires.  These sires score high in terms of backfat, loin-eye and days-to-240 pounds. They are raised on dirt lots, and fed via 80-bushel feeders, and are durable and tough!

Some of our young pigs.

For more than 50 years, we have raised both purebred and crossbred breeding stock in central Nebraska. Our boars, gilts and Show Pigs have sold all over the nation to satisfied customers.
